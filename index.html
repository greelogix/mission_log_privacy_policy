01 / INTRODUCTION

Introduction

Welcome to GTA 6 Tracker, an independent, unofficial gaming companion.

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA 6 Tracker handles information when you use our mobile application.

GTA 6 Tracker is designed around privacy. The app does not require registration, does not provide cloud synchronization, and stores your gaming records locally on your device.

GTA 6 Tracker is an independent, unofficial companion application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and GTA are trademarks of their respective owners. By using GTA 6 Tracker, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

02 / INFORMATION

Information We Collect

GTA 6 Tracker does not require you to provide personal information to create an account because the application does not have an account or registration system.

The following information may be entered and stored locally when you use the app:

  • Game profile names and platform selections.
  • Mission, achievement, collectible, and custom goal records.
  • Checklist tasks, categories, and completion statuses.
  • Journal titles, notes, dates, and tags.
  • Optional gameplay session durations.
  • Application preferences, including accent color.
  • Local progress and recent activity records.

Important: These are user-created application records. GTA 6 Tracker stores them locally and does not upload them to our servers.

03 / LOCAL STORAGE

How Your Information Is Stored

GTA 6 Tracker uses on-device storage, including SharedPreferences, to save your application data and preferences.

Your records remain within the app's local storage rather than being synchronized with a GTA 6 Tracker cloud account or backend server.

GTA 6 Tracker does not provide an account-based backup or recovery service. If you uninstall the application or clear its app data, locally stored records may be permanently lost.

Device operating system backup behavior may be governed by your device settings and platform policies.

04 / DATA SHARING

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not receive or sell your locally stored gaming records through GTA 6 Tracker.

We do not upload your missions, achievements, checklists, journal entries, or gameplay sessions to our servers.

We do not share this locally stored content with advertisers or data brokers.

If you independently share information outside the app, that sharing is subject to the services and settings you choose to use.

05 / DEVICE PERMISSIONS

Device Permissions

GTA 6 Tracker is designed to provide its core tracking and journaling functionality without requiring an online account or server connection.

If a future version introduces features that require additional device permissions, this policy will be updated to explain their purpose and how they are used.

06 / DATA RETENTION

Data Retention and Deletion

Your app records are retained in local device storage until you remove them or remove the application's data.

You can remove your information by:

  • Using the Clear All Data option in GTA 6 Tracker Settings.
  • Clearing GTA 6 Tracker's storage through your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the application.

Clearing all data is permanent and removes your saved game profiles, tracker records, checklist tasks, journal entries, sessions, and related local activity.

We do not maintain a server-side copy of your GTA 6 Tracker records that can be restored after deletion.

07 / CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Children's Privacy

GTA 6 Tracker does not require users to submit personal information or create an account.

The application is not designed to collect personal information from children through an account or registration process.

Parents or guardians who have questions about the application's privacy practices can contact us using the details below.

08 / SECURITY

Data Security

GTA 6 Tracker is designed to minimize data exposure by keeping user-created gaming records in local application storage instead of transmitting them to our servers.

However, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Device security, operating system protections, and physical access to your device remain important.

We recommend keeping your device protected with an appropriate screen lock and operating system updates.

09 / THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Third-Party Services

GTA 6 Tracker's core functionality does not require third-party accounts, cloud synchronization, or advertising services.

The application is distributed through third-party app stores. Google Play and Apple App Store may independently process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The hosting provider for this Privacy Policy website may also process standard website request information under its own privacy practices. This is separate from the local data stored inside GTA 6 Tracker.
